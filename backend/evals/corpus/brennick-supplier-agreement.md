# Supplier Agreement Summary — Brennick Plastics

Prepared by Procurement for internal reference. The signed contract (ref. HV-PROC-2026-014) prevails over this summary.

## Parties

Halvard Instruments B.V. and Brennick Plastics Ltd., Shannon, Ireland. Brennick account manager: Siobhán Gallagher.

## Scope

Brennick supplies injection-moulded housings in glass-filled polycarbonate for the Tidewater product line, initially for the TW-400 sensor, including end caps and O-ring grooves machined to Halvard drawing TW4-H-200 rev. C.

## Term

The agreement runs for 3 years from 1 March 2026 and renews automatically for 1-year periods unless either party gives notice.

## Pricing and volumes

- Unit price: €11.20 per housing set (housing and two end caps) for the first year, then indexed to the European polymer price index, capped at ±5 % per year.
- Minimum order quantity (MOQ): 5,000 housing sets per order.
- Lead time: 6 weeks from purchase order.
- Annual forecast: Halvard shares a rolling 12-month forecast every quarter.

## Payment

Payment terms are net 45 days from invoice.

## Quality

Brennick is ISO 9001 certified. Each batch ships with a pressure-test certificate for 25 bar. The accepted defect rate is below 0.5 %; above that Halvard may reject the whole batch.

## Late delivery

For late delivery, Brennick pays a penalty of 2 % of the order value per full week of delay, capped at 10 % of the order value.

## Termination

Either party may terminate for convenience with 120 days' written notice. Halvard may terminate immediately if two consecutive batches fail quality inspection.
