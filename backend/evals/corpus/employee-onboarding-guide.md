# Welcome to Halvard Instruments — Onboarding Guide

This guide is for new employees in their first 90 days. Your manager and your onboarding buddy will help you with everything below.

## Before your first day

IT orders your laptop at least 5 working days before your start date. You choose between a 14-inch Linux laptop and a 14-inch macOS laptop. Your account details arrive by text message on the Friday before you start.

## Your first week

- Day 1: welcome session with People Operations at 09:30, collect your laptop and badge.
- You are assigned an onboarding buddy from another team; meet them in your first week.
- Complete the security awareness training in your first week. It is mandatory, takes about 45 minutes, and your access to production systems is not granted until it is done.
- Set up the company VPN, Halvard Connect, and multi-factor authentication with a hardware key (provided by IT).

## Tools

- Chat: Slack. Join #general, #announcements and your team channel.
- Expenses: Ledgerline. Travel booking: Wayfarer.
- Documents: the company wiki.
- Paging (engineers on call only): Beacon.

## Working arrangements

Halvard is hybrid: employees are in the office at least two days a week, on days agreed with their team. The offices are in Gdańsk (headquarters), Rotterdam and Lisbon. Core hours are 10:00–15:00 local time.

## Time off

Full-time employees have 26 days of paid holiday per year plus public holidays of their office's country. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of €1,200 per year for courses, books and conferences. Ask your manager before spending more than €400 at once.

## Your 90 days

You have check-ins with your manager after 30, 60 and 90 days. The 90-day check-in ends the probation period. People Operations sends a short survey after 90 days about your onboarding.
