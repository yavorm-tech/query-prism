# Halvard Instruments — Travel and Expense Policy

Version 4.2, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance Operations (Marta Oyelaran, Head of Finance Operations).

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains what Halvard Instruments pays for when employees travel on company business, how to book, and how to claim expenses. It applies to all employees and contractors with a Halvard email address.

## 2. Booking travel

All flights, trains and hotels are booked through the company travel portal, Wayfarer. Bookings made outside Wayfarer are reimbursed only if they were at least 15 % cheaper than the Wayfarer price shown on the same day; attach a screenshot of the Wayfarer price to the claim.

### 2.1 Flights

- Flights with a scheduled flight time under 6 hours are booked in economy class.
- Flights of 6 hours or more may be booked in business class with the written approval of a Vice President (VP) of the traveller's division. Without that approval, economy applies.
- Premium economy is allowed on any flight of 4 hours or more without extra approval.
- Checked baggage is reimbursed for trips of three nights or more, or when carrying demonstration equipment.

### 2.2 Rail

Rail is preferred over flying for journeys under 4 hours door to door. First class rail is allowed when the journey is longer than 2 hours.

### 2.3 Hotels

The nightly hotel cap is €180 including taxes. Exceptions: London, Zurich and New York have a cap of €240 per night. Travellers who stay with friends or family instead of a hotel may claim a flat €35 per night.

## 3. Daily allowance (per diem)

Halvard pays a per diem of €65 per full travel day to cover meals and incidentals. On the departure and return days the per diem is €40. If a meal is provided (for example at a conference), deduct €15 for lunch and €20 for dinner.

## 4. Ground transport

- Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed between airports or stations and the hotel or client site, and after 21:00.
- Rental cars need manager approval and must be compact class unless four or more employees travel together.
- Private car use is reimbursed at €0.30 per kilometre, plus tolls and parking.

## 5. Claiming expenses

Expenses are claimed in Ledgerline, the expense tool. Claims, with itemised receipts, must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted later than 90 days after the trip are not reimbursed. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll after approval.

Alcohol is not reimbursed except at client dinners approved in advance by a director. Upgrades bought with personal loyalty points are allowed; the points themselves are not reimbursed.

## 6. Corporate card

Employees who travel more than six times a year receive a Halvard corporate card with a monthly limit of €5,000. Personal use of the corporate card is not allowed; any personal charge must be repaid within 10 days.
